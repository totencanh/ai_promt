# Visual style analysis

Observations are based on the full 3-second timeline contact sheets and representative stills in `frames/`.

- **Illustration:** deliberately simple, flat 2D shapes; blocky silhouettes, minimal detail, slight hand-drawn irregularity in outlines. Staging resembles cut-paper/limited-animation art. This describes appearance, not the software or production method.
- **Characters:** compact, simplified bodies with small torsos/robe-like shapes and oversized pale oval faces. Faces use very large black eyes and tiny mouth shapes; identity and emotion are carried by costume color, head shape, and a few expression changes.
- **Line art:** dark, heavy outlines against filled shapes; limited interior line detail.
- **Palette:** night scenes use near-black navy/green, muted brown/ochre and dark teal; water is desaturated blue; village/temple exteriors add dusty gray-green skies and pale ground; red architecture and purple bedding provide stronger accents.
- **Lighting/shadows:** darkness is largely represented by dark fields rather than modeled gradients. A small warm lamp and circular glow/halo marks create local light cues. Ground shadows are simple flat ellipses. There is little observed volumetric shading.
- **Backgrounds:** economical symbolic sets (bed and window, doorway, house row, waterline, stall, temple façade, well, statue). Large empty upper areas hold narration text and support a somber tone.
- **Composition:** phone-vertical 9:16. Figures are often small within wide tableaux; a few close-ups, particularly the water face at 69 s and 129–136 s, strengthen emphasis. Strong horizontal horizon/shore bands organize water scenes. Threats are often placed opposite the protagonist or isolated against darkness.
- **Typography:** small white sans-serif-like Vietnamese captions, centered near the top with dark backing/negative space. Legibility is moderate at native resolution and shrinks in the contact sheets. No clear title treatment or typographic hierarchy was observed in sampled frames.

For an original system, retain the high-contrast shapes, limited palette, sparse symbolic sets, expressive face-to-body scale, and safe caption area while creating distinct designs, motifs, and color logic.
