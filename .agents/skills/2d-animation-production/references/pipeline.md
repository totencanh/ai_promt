# Production pipeline quick reference

The complete source of truth is the root [`MASTER_PRODUCTION_GUIDE.md`](../../../../MASTER_PRODUCTION_GUIDE.md). Use this page only as a compact stage map; if guidance conflicts, follow the master.

| # | Stage | Key output | Move forward when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Idea | `story.md` | Hook, stakes, causal logic, and ending are clear. |
| 2 | Script | `script.md` | Narration/dialogue and line IDs are reviewed; timing is still provisional. |
| 3 | Scene breakdown | `scene-plan.md` | Every script line and story beat maps to scene IDs. |
| 4 | Storyboard | `storyboard.md` | Composition, action, motion, caption, transition, and audio cues are specified. |
| 5 | Character design | `character-bible.md` and sheet | Identity and silhouette have been reviewed at output size. |
| 6 | Environment design | `environment-bible.md` and keyframe | Layout, lighting, depth, palette, and continuity are established. |
| 7 | Visual assets | `assets/` | A representative style-locked proof is reviewed before batch production. |
| 8 | Voice | measured VO files and mapping | Rights, reference, engine, pronunciation, and real duration are checked. |
| 9 | Sound | audio cues and `audio-plan.md` | Cues have sources/licenses and the mix has been auditioned. |
| 10 | Animation | scene renders | Intended movement matches the board and is reviewed; do not call a slideshow character animation. |
| 11 | Edit | timeline and captions | Timings use measured media; picture and sound are synchronized. |
| 12 | QC | `qc-report.md` | Full export is watched and heard; defects are fixed or documented as blockers. |
| 13 | Export | final file and metadata | Decode and technical checks pass, with artistic approval recorded separately. |

For a sample or long-form request, use the proof-of-concept and scale gates in the master guide. A blocked downstream tool does not block independent planning; label the missing stage and its dependency precisely.
