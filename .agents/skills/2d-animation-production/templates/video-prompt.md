# Video prompt assembly

Use approved scene art as a visual source. Preserve character identity, costume, proportions, linework, palette, and environment layout.

## Start state
Describe the exact initial pose, prop state, and composition.

## Motion, in order
Describe one primary readable action, including body/facial movement, prop interaction, and environmental motion only where intended.

## Camera
Static or one motivated camera move. Do not add unrequested cuts, reframing, or shake.

## Timing
- 0–2 s:
- 2–5 s:
- 5–8 s:

Adjust intervals to the actual shot duration and planned beat.

## End state
Describe the final pose and prop/environment state needed for continuity into the next shot.

## Motion limits
No morphing, identity drift, extra objects, random gestures, lip movement without dialogue, or unmotivated camera movement.

Record the tool/model/version, source frames, prompt, seed when available, output path, and review status. A prompt is not a rendered or reviewed shot.
