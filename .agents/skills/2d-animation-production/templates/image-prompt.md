# Image prompt assembly

## Project style
Approved Style Bible: [version/path]. Describe only its established visual language.

## Character identity
Use [CHAR-ID] and its approved reference. Preserve silhouette, proportions, face, hair, costume, palette, and signature props.

## Environment
Use [ENV-ID] and its approved keyframe. Preserve layout, architecture, lighting direction, palette, and established objects.

## Shot
Shot size, angle, composition, subject placement, focal point, and target aspect ratio.

## Pose and emotion
One readable pose/action and a precise facial expression.

## Light, atmosphere, and color
Specify scene-specific light, shadows, atmosphere, and approved palette accents.

## Caption-safe area
Reserve [region]. Do not generate text unless explicitly requested.

## Continuity
Match [previous approved scene state / prop state] where relevant.

## Negative constraints
No identity or costume drift, duplicated figures, anatomy defects, unrequested objects, random text, watermark, photorealism/3D when excluded by the style, or unrequested gore. Adapt the negative prompt to the selected model; omit the field if unsupported.

Record prompt, model/tool/version/seed when available, reference paths, source/license, and review status with the resulting asset.
